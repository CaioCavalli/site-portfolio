Description

I worked as a Data Analyst with a focus on predictive analyses based on clinical and financial data to prioritize high-risk patients. I performed analyses of high-cost patients and providers in SQL and Power BI, with a focus on expense control. I developed ETL routines to cleanse, standardize, and integrate around 5,000 clinical records, structuring consistent datasets for analysis. I improved preventive alert reports with parameterized SQL and automated quality validations, integrated the Scamalytics API into fraud-prevention flows, and implemented logging and traceability in data pipelines, reducing incidents and increasing visibility for technical and business areas. I collaborated with technical and non-technical stakeholders to evolve analytical flows and interfaces.
